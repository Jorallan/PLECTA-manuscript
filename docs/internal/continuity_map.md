## Recognisable scientific arc to preserve

1. Dense crossing filaments are difficult to separate into instances.
2. A semantic mask does not encode filament identity.
3. The binary axis mask contains crossings, contacts, breaks, and locally
   ambiguous continuations.
4. The proposed method decomposes that mask and reconnects its pieces using
   explicit geometric and topological evidence.
5. Procedural scenes provide controlled instance-level truth.
6. CNT SEM demonstrates the intended application.
7. Results establish performance, robustness, and limitations.

The default section order remains Introduction, Materials and methods, Results,
Discussion, and Conclusions.
