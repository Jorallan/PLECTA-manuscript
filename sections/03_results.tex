\section{\rev{Results}}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{\rev{Held-out synthetic reconstruction}}
\label{sec:res-density}

\rev{PLECTA completed all \PlectaHeldoutSceneCount{} held-out scenes with
\PlectaHeldoutFailureCount{} failures. Mean common-fragment pairwise
\Fone{} was \PlectaHeldoutFOneMean{} (density-stratified 95\% bootstrap CI
[\PlectaHeldoutFOneCILow{}, \PlectaHeldoutFOneCIHigh{}]); mean precision,
recall, reference-instance recovery, and adjusted Rand index were
\PlectaHeldoutPrecisionMean{}, \PlectaHeldoutRecallMean{},
\PlectaHeldoutRecoveryMean{}, and \PlectaHeldoutARIMean{}, respectively
(Table~\ref{tab:plecta-heldout}). These scenes used unseen seeds but came from
the same procedural generator as development data, so the result measures
same-generator generalisation rather than distribution transfer.}

\rev{As a diagnostic floor, assigning one instance label to every connected
component of the same held-out masks produced $F_1=
\PlectaCCFloorFOne{}$ (precision \PlectaCCFloorPrecision{}, recall
\PlectaCCFloorRecall{}, ARI \PlectaCCFloorARI{}). Its high recall but very low
precision and VI-merge of \PlectaCCFloorVIMerge{} show the expected failure:
crossings collapse many distinct filaments into a single component. This is not
a competitive tracer, but it verifies that connectivity alone cannot solve the
evaluated grouping task.}

\rev{Performance decreased with scene density (Figure~\ref{fig:plecta-performance}a).
Mean \Fone{} fell from \PlectaDensityTwoZeroFOneMean{}
[\PlectaDensityTwoZeroFOneCILow{}, \PlectaDensityTwoZeroFOneCIHigh{}] at 20\% coverage
to \PlectaDensitySixZeroFOneMean{}
[\PlectaDensitySixZeroFOneCILow{}, \PlectaDensitySixZeroFOneCIHigh{}] at 60\% coverage;
the intermediate 30\% and 40\% means were \PlectaDensityThreeZeroFOneMean{} and
\PlectaDensityFourZeroFOneMean{}. Recorded per-scene runtime had mean
\PlectaHeldoutRuntimeMean{}~s, median \PlectaHeldoutRuntimeMedian{}~s,
90th percentile \PlectaHeldoutRuntimePNinety{}~s, and maximum
\PlectaHeldoutRuntimeMaximum{}~s; these timings are hardware-specific.}

behaviour rather than only aggregate scores. Most fragments are assigned
consistently in the sparse scene. In the dense scene, the longer red runs and
reduced recovery expose both erroneous unions and interrupted identities around
crowded junctions; the prediction nevertheless retains explicit overlap at
crossings instead of forcing a single flat label.}

\subsection{\rev{Input-mask robustness}}
\label{sec:res-mask}

\rev{On the separate \PlectaRobustnessSceneCount{}-scene controlled set,
mean \Fone{} was \PlectaRobustnessDegradedFOneMean{} from degraded axes and
\PlectaRobustnessCleanFOneMean{} from the corresponding clean axes. The paired
clean-minus-degraded change was \PlectaCleanMinusDegradedFOneMean{} (95\% CI
[\PlectaCleanMinusDegradedFOneCILow{},
\PlectaCleanMinusDegradedFOneCIHigh{}]). Thus the modelled gaps and raster
irregularities caused a small resolved loss under this generator, without
testing robustness to a different image distribution.}

\subsection{\rev{Development ablations and downstream rendering}}
\label{sec:res-ablation}
\label{sec:res-factorial}

\rev{One-at-a-time ablations were evaluated only on the
\PlectaDevelopmentSceneCount{} development scenes
(Table~\ref{tab:plecta-ablation}; Figure~\ref{fig:plecta-performance}b).
Relative to the evaluated configuration, removing the junction chord-turn term
changed \Fone{} by \PlectaAblationTangentTermOnlyDeltaFOne{}, removing gap
matching by \PlectaAblationNoGapBridgingDeltaFOne{}, and disabling connector-
based junction-cluster consolidation by
\PlectaAblationNoCrossingMergingAtAllDeltaFOne{}. Removing chain-extended
frames and using a single round produced smaller changes of
\PlectaAblationNoChainExtendedFramesDeltaFOne{} and
\PlectaAblationSingleRoundDeltaFOne{}. These development results identify
chord-aware continuation, gap matching, and junction-topology preconditioning
as the largest tested contributors; they are not held-out effect estimates.}

\rev{Default Stage~4 ribbon rendering achieved development \Fone{}=
\PlectaStageFourFOne{}, a change of \PlectaStageFourDeltaFOne{} from the core
centreline output. The rendered instances had disconnected-component fraction
\PlectaStageFourDisconnected{} and branched fraction
\PlectaStageFourBranched{}. Rendering therefore improved mask continuity while
slightly changing the representation score, without changing chain membership.
Enabling the separate 0.6 silhouette-absorption rule reduced \Fone{} further
to \PlectaStageFourAbsorbFOne{}. Because absorption can merge identities, it
remained disabled and neither rendering condition contributed to the held-out
grouping result. These topology measurements do not validate apparent width.}

\rev{The optional image-characterisation stage was separately checked against
the known synthetic renderer on \PlectaWidthValidationSceneCount{} development
scenes. Among \PlectaWidthValidationInstanceCount{} instances matched to one
reference filament with purity at least 0.9, fitted apparent width had Pearson
$r=\PlectaWidthValidationPearson{}$, mean absolute error
\PlectaWidthValidationMAE{} px, and median absolute error
\PlectaWidthValidationMedianAE{} px. A quality-stratified 90\% interval,
calibrated by leaving scenes out, covered \PlectaWidthValidationCoverage{} of
instances. This validates recovery of the synthetic renderer's width under the
development model; it is not a validation of physical CNT diameter.}

\subsection{\rev{Two-field CNT SEM mask-source case}}
\label{sec:res-real}
\label{sec:res-qualitative}

\rev{The two real SEM fields were exploratory development cases
(Table~\ref{tab:plecta-real-masks}). From the manual-derived axis, PLECTA
reached \Fone{}=\PlectaBFiveEightOneZeroZeroManualFOne{} on B58\_100 and
\PlectaBFiveEightOneOneZeroManualFOne{} on B58\_110. These are oracle-input conditions
because the axes were obtained from the manual instance annotations. With the
upstream U-Net axes, the corresponding values were
\PlectaBFiveEightOneZeroZeroUNetFOne{} and \PlectaBFiveEightOneOneZeroUNetFOne{}. The U-Net axis had
recall/precision \PlectaBFiveEightOneZeroZeroAxisRecall{}/\PlectaBFiveEightOneZeroZeroAxisPrecision{} on
B58\_100 and \PlectaBFiveEightOneOneZeroAxisRecall{}/\PlectaBFiveEightOneOneZeroAxisPrecision{} on
B58\_110.}
