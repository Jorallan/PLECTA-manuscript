\section{Independent centreline-density and bundle-width factorial}
\label{app:factorial}

The original generator stops on thick-mask areal coverage, which couples total
centreline length and physical width. A separate development generator instead
stops on summed visible centreline arc length. It generated a 3-by-3 grid of
target length densities and physical bundle widths with four disjoint seeds per
cell. For each seed and length-density block, the degraded one-pixel input,
clean centreline and overlap-aware centreline ground truth were byte-identical
across all three physical-width cells. Width therefore acts as a structural
negative control for Stage~3 and as an active factor only for SEM rendering and
Stage~4.

Increasing centreline density raised crossing density and gap count, while
increasing physical width at fixed centreline geometry raised thick-mask
coverage. The report records the full achieved width distribution, one-pixel
coverage, degraded-mask coverage, filament count, pairwise and unique crossing
events, and gap-length summaries. Empirical Stage-3 execution was not repeated
across the redundant width cells; the negative-control result here is exact
artifact identity, not an estimated performance effect.

\section{Dataset identity}
\label{app:data}

The development set contains 20 procedural geometries and the single real crop.
The disjoint predefined set was generated with base seed 20260801 and 25 scenes at
each prescribed coverage. For density $d\in\{20,30,40,50,60\}$ and zero-based
index $i$, the seed was $20260801+100d+i$. Thus the original predefined ranges were
20262801--20262825, 20263801--20263825, 20264801--20264825,
20265801--20265825 and 20266801--20266825. If the predefined precision rule
required extension at a density, indices beginning at 25 were appended without
replacing an existing scene. Exact scene paths, seeds, input and ground-truth
hashes, method outputs and failure states are preserved in
\texttt{evaluation\_manifest.csv}.
