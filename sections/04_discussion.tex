\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
\label{sec:discussion}
\label{sec:limitations}

PLECTA reconstructs overlapping strand instances from a binary strand-axis
mask using deterministic junction matching that permits both continuations and
genuine endpoints. Its grouping core requires no training data and produces
identical output from identical input masks. The upstream segmenter is trained
on synthetic image--mask pairs, so the full workflow requires no manually
annotated training micrographs.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,label=\textbullet,topsep=6pt,itemsep=2pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item Across \PlectaHeldoutSceneCount{} synthetic scenes, PLECTA achieved mean pairwise
        \Fone{}=\PlectaHeldoutFOneMean{} [\PlectaHeldoutFOneCILow{},
        \PlectaHeldoutFOneCIHigh{}], falling from
        \PlectaDensityTwoZeroFOneMean{} to \PlectaDensitySixZeroFOneMean{} as
        areal coverage rises from 20 to 60\,\%.
  \item In matched-mask comparisons, PLECTA achieved the highest scores among
        the evaluated methods across the tested densities. Its lead was also
        retained on GraFT's released generator at published settings.
  \item The ablation analysis identified the junction chord-turn term and
        gap bridging as the largest contributors to reconstruction
        performance.
  \item The greyscale depth stage ordered crossings that it decided with
        \PlectaDepthDecidedLow{}--\PlectaDepthDecidedHigh{} accuracy, against
        chance level \PlectaDepthChance{}. Its global order is limited to
        strands connected through crossings.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Scope and limitations.}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,label=\textbullet,topsep=6pt,itemsep=2pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item PLECTA assumes directional continuity through crossings and remains
        sensitive to upstream mask errors. It can bridge short gaps but cannot
        recover omitted strands or reliably correct false axes.
  \item The real-material evaluation is a case study of
        \PlectaRealFieldCountLower{} CNT SEM fields. The automatic-axis mean
        \Fone{} of \PlectaRealUNetFOneMean{} lies within the direct
        inter-annotator agreement range of
        \PlectaInterAnnotatorAgreeFLow{}--\PlectaInterAnnotatorAgreeFHigh{},
        so it should be interpreted alongside ambiguity in defining strand
        identity through local occlusions.
  \item The comparisons evaluate overlap-aware grouping of matched binary
        masks in the tested density regimes. They support the value of
        PLECTA's combination of junction-level matching, endpoint handling
        and gap bridging, but do not establish performance in every method's
        native application.
\end{itemize}

Broader validation will require independent real-image test sets across
materials and imaging conditions, validated physical-width measurements and
further matched implementations of competing methods.
